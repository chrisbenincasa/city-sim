The city in space — roads, Lots, Trips and the first commute

Five milestones over six days that gave the city a Road Graph, Lots derived rather than painted, a Trip model that decomposes into Legs, a job to walk to and a car to drive there in. One of them carried the roadmap's only risk marked irreversible — and it was retired by a structure rather than by the mechanism it was scoped around.

What this page is, and two caveats that govern every number on it

Assembled from plans/0020–plans/0026, plus the two spikes underneath them. Every fact here is owned somewhere else; where this page disagrees, those documents win.

ACT 2

What planning found

A conclusion withdrawn on the day it shipped, three recommendations refused, and a precondition nobody had finished counting

5a

The slice's own headline conclusion was withdrawn by amendment on the day it shipped

It first claimed the shipped road geometry "sits well inside the regime where the dial works". The amendment: "that is false, and the shipped Ruleset severs nothing at all." The cause is stated as a rule — "two observations differing in two variables support no claim about either" — and the re-derivation found the real mechanism: the dial states a ratio, and what reconnects a city is an absolute count. Severance appears at 16 crossings and not at 35, on both axes.

5a

The diagnostic announced Severance over a city that has none, for the whole slice

Its verdict compared foot components against car components, and the shipped world reads 66 against 8 because a motorway lays its own junction nodes — 65 of the 66 are motorway junctions. The test found this on day one and filtered it out with a predicate, and the predicate never left the test file. "A predicate discovered in a test and left there is a correction with a blast radius of one call site."

5a-bis

Three of the brief's five recommendations were wrong, and one of them was wrong from correct quotations

The recommendation of one grid-snapped Street Tile per command rests on two accurate quotations — but the generator puts nodes only at grid intersections a block apart, so one Segment spans a whole block face and there is no such thing as a Street Tile in the graph the previous milestone built. The sitting's own finding: a brief is evidence about what its author could see, not a specification.

5a-bis

A second recommendation dissolved because the derivation was already written down

The brief predicted "two or more hash-bearing world-creation numbers". It is one, and it was already in the vocabulary document: five Buildings share a Segment. Measured: the graph gives 33,024 Street Segments against that document's ~30,000, by construction rather than by arrangement — so the Lot count is 165,120, against an independently-chosen 225,000 at a million. Two figures that never met, agreeing within a quarter. Depth was deleted rather than chosen.

5b

The two ADRs governing the pedestrian layer asked for a structure a third rejects by name

One said the road network needs sidewalk edges alongside street edges and crossing edges at junctions; every other document says the opposite. Resolved by splitting rather than substituting: the sidewalk half refused, the crossing half standing literally as foot-only Segments. "An and in a consequence is two consequences, and it should be written as two."

5c

A precondition stated in the singular is a precondition nobody has finished counting

"5b task 4's generator had no destination set, 5b's volume had no path, and now the path has no vehicle." Then a fourth arrived mid-milestone: the vehicles with no driver. Mode choice is classified undesigned rather than unbuilt — "an inventory of unplaced mechanisms structurally cannot list a mechanism nobody designed."

And one finding that reaches past this whole group

A generated city cannot congest itself. The paved extent scales with the square root of population, so the network grows with the traffic — "the same number sizes both the demand and the supply." That is why 5c's acceptance run, the named ratifier for four hash-bearing numbers, ran and could not fire. It has since been sighted six times across the project under different names.

ACT 3

What shipped

A Trip that decomposes, a Leg that stores a cost and not a path, and the arrow that had to wait a milestone

The walk search, and the unit that turned out to be durable

Measured on the shipped lattice — 16,700 nodes and 32,890 Segments, which is the million-Citizen city's graph and not a scaled-down stand-in. Cost runs from 16.8 ns for a walk across the street to 38.4 µs at 4.1 km: a span of 446×, at a flat 35–40 ns per settled node throughout, allocating nothing at any rung.

So the durable unit is the settled node, and the search costs roughly 37 ns × distance². ⚠ Which is also the warning: 464 routes at 512 m is 1.0% of a Tick at 4×, and at 4.1 km one route on its own is 114%.

The shape of the answer is the finding

Severance is a tail, not a median. On the demonstration file the medians barely move — but 135,767 Building pairs had no pedestrian route at all, against 64,233 that did, and not one of the longest band's 1,170 pairs had a route. ⚠ Those minute figures are on the old clock and were later found short by up to one Tick.

Driving, priced on entry — and a repair that changed every congestion figure

A drive is priced Segment by Segment as it is met. Between the demonstration and the acceptance run, a defect was found in the advance loop: it handled each Traveller once per call, so a drive crossed one Segment a Tick whatever its free-flow cost — a journey quoted at 2.10 Ticks took 11.66.

The capacity sweep's congestion bills went from ×1.0088 to ×1.1925 at one rung and from ×1.8949 to ×7.0869 at the next. ⚠ The repair moved no State Hash at all, for a reason worth keeping: "no committed baseline in this project contains a single drive."

The largest finding of the last milestone, in one sentence

The priced and free-flow runs agree to the Citizen on who is employed and on every rung count, at every rung — while their occupancies differ by half. That is the routing decision working exactly as decided, and it is also the corpus sweep's the traffic model has no feedback term arriving with a number attached. Congestion is a cost paid and never a cost avoided.

The route cache, and a policy chosen against the textbook

Serving a cached route costs 0.525 µs against 12.349 µs to search — 23.5×. ⚠ "That figure is a property of the coverage and not of the cache." At 16,000 Citizens with 4,808 distinct pairs and a 4,096-entry store, the hit rates are: ceiling 85.19%, LRU 36.68%, MRU 72.42%, random 58.30%.

LRU is the worst real policy here, and the reason is structural: a commute is a once-per-Day cyclic scan, so over a working set larger than the store, LRU evicts exactly the entry needed next. The default is MRU.

ACT 4

What is still open

Six unset numbers, all behind one undesigned model — and a ratifier that ran, could not fire, and amended the rule about ratifiers

§D2

Six hash-bearing numbers are unset, and every one waits on the same missing model

The Microscopic Cap, the Temperament base and spread, the Rejoin crossing budget, the number of Habit Route variants, the Aggravation threshold, and the Habit staleness bound. All six sit behind the driver model, which the roadmap parks deliberately unnumbered. ⚠ And the consequence is live: the build prices congestion on entry and routes on free flow, which is exactly the configuration the routing ADR refuses by name — so there is no feedback term, and all six numbers plus the budget ledger's largest row are denominated in the mechanism that would supply one.

5c

A named ratifier ran, could not fire, and produced an amendment to the rule that named it

The acceptance run was the ratifier for four hash-bearing numbers. Load came out at 0.0018 / 0.0048 / 0.0110 Vehicles per Segment per Tick at 4,000 / 16,000 / 64,000 Citizens, growing as roughly P^0.66 — extrapolating to ~0.07 at a million against a Segment holding 1.02 on the congested file. The result is an amendment: a named ratifier names a machine and a world — and then a third clause, and a quantity, because one number's two refuting readings both fired and refuted the wrong thing.

5b-bis

Five ratifiers ran, none of the stated refuting numbers occurred, and none was ticked

"Whether that promotes any of them from unratified is a designer's decision and this milestone declined to take it." ⚠ And one of the five is stated in a form the run showed cannot be measured — its row "needs amending rather than ticking." That number was retired outright two days later, when a later ADR found it was a restatement of a quantity already measured.

5b-bis

The Commute Budget binds hard, and more than half the city's declared posts are unreachable

At 10,000 Citizens over 100,000 Ticks: 4,561 hold a job against 9,608 posts declared, and jobs beyond budget is never zero. ⚠ The cause is not scarcity — there is no land use in this city at all. Every Building has the same eight posts and the same three occupants, so 222 blocks of 228 come out within a quarter of parity.

5b-bis

A distribution censored by a number is not evidence about that number

A commute exists only because the assignment pass already accepted the job at the other end of it, inside the Budget. So at a one-minute Budget every commute the fixture makes is under a minute — the distribution collapses into its shortest band rather than growing a tail. This is why the Budget's own ratification cannot come from the mechanism it gates.

5b-bis

The job-search box is derived from the Budget at walking speed, and a driver gets a pedestrian's catchment

Inert today — the box holds 100.0% of Buildings up to about 160,000 Citizens — and silently clipping the moment a city outgrows it.

⚠ instr

A defect that only shows on a value you happen to know is a defect that hides in every value you do not

The Trip dump's minute formatter dropped the sub-Tick fraction before converting, so every figure that instrument had ever printed was short by up to one Tick. It surfaced only because the dump prints the Commute Budget in its own header and the shipped 20 came out as 19.9. On the band that a crossing-cost ratifier is read off, the error is 7%.
