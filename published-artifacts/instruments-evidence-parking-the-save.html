Instruments — Evidence, parking, the save, and a field nothing set

Four milestones that built the things which let somebody find out what the simulation did: a trail that survives the Building it describes, a parking model that diagnoses rather than averages, a save that computes its own State Hash, and the land value target six other decisions were already reading. They are also where the corpus kept catching itself believing its own descriptions.

What this page is, and the one hazard it is written against

Assembled from plans/0028, 0030, 0031, 0032 and 0034. Every fact here is owned somewhere else; where this page disagrees, those documents win.

Figures carry the clause naming what they measure. ⚠ Several sources omit their own conditions — a Citizen count, a machine class, a build configuration. Those are marked rather than given borrowed ones from a neighbouring number, which is the failure mode this corpus files under Cause 5.

The through-line across all four

The corpus's rule is that a description of the build tells you where to look and never what you found. These four milestones are where that kept costing something: a build diagnostic that told every developer a serialiser existed when it never had; a green negative test that pinned a wrong conclusion three documents then cited; doc comments wrong by 9× and 12× with nothing able to catch them; and four sentences saying a Tick was a quarter of its real length, one of them printed to the operator.

ACT 2

What planning found

Two ADRs each putting a thing on an object that cannot hold it, a term that was never a weight, and a repair that would have been the defect

7

The thing that holds a parking space did not exist, and two ADRs each put it somewhere impossible

One said a Trip remembers where it parked; the other said the Traveller carries it — and called the agreement corroboration. Both are freed at the end of the journey, and the canonical case is "a household's car sits at home overnight." The live consequence: that ADR's conservation sum reads 0 = high every night. "It was writable only because the state did not exist — which is that ADR's own argument working against it."

7

The brief's own recommended repair would have been the defect the invariant exists to catch

It recommended hanging the space on the Household. But every member of a car-owning Household drives, "so a Household of three workers parks three cars, and one column would overwrite two of them" — which is the permanent capacity loss this milestone's risk is about. It went on the Citizen.

9

A term in the desirability formula was never a weight, and no document had ever said so

The formula has land value as one of five weighted terms, and the same section says land value moves slowly toward the current desirability. Substituting one into the other: that term is a gain on the other four, divergent at 1 or above. "A term that appears on both sides of a lag is a gain, and calling it a weight hides which way the loop runs." ⚠ It was findable at any point since the Layer shipped — both halves have been in adjacent paragraphs the whole time. "A formula nobody evaluates is not checked by being read."

8

The risk had a cheaper instrument all along, and got scheduled behind the test it was named for

Two existing calls measure it "directly, immediately, and with no save format." "A risk named after the test that would catch it gets scheduled behind that test." Existing coverage at the time: 2 of 28 derived columns.

7

A milestone renumber left seven traps in the source that no check in this corpus can see

Eight code comments named the same milestone number; seven meant parking and one meant save/load. "They are the same string." "A milestone number is neither a symbol nor a time, and it is the one citation form that goes stale without anybody editing the thing it cites." The eventual audit found 73 sites naming a milestone, 14 of them stale — and only one of the fourteen wanted a new number.

6

The accumulator went inside the world, reversing the session's own earlier recommendation

Two findings killed the split. An unhashed buffer written in the movement phase is the banned parallel-accumulate — and the thread-count lint cannot see it, because it compares State Hashes. "The buffer would scramble and every test would stay green." And for the canonical case there is no entity to hang a count on: a departed Household is gone.

ACT 3

What shipped

And the sharpest of it is a mechanism that was written down as impossible, by a test that was about something else

And the sharpest finding is about how the wrong claim was pinned

The impossible conclusion was held in place by a test — one asserting that a fold over the bytes is not the State Hash. It is still green, and it was always about something else: it folds the buffer flat, byte after byte, "which is a thing nobody would ever want." Three documents cited it and none of them opened it. "A negative test is the most quotable kind of all, because it reads as a closed door."

Parking — a sweep that ratified one dial, refuted another and amended a third

A ninth Ruleset was authored first: the shipped world with per-kind parking cut and every Household given a car. Then seven rungs, four whole Days, 4,000 Citizens, probing every Building door.

One dial is refuted. The shed's cap keeps 24 Car Parks, and the room-past-the-cap column equals the exhausted column at every rung — it is too small, in exactly the direction the ledger predicted. No successor value was set. "A dial refuted while it is hash-neutral is a debt with a due date, not a defect."

And the radius's ratifying clause had to be amended, because the world defeated the instrument

Occupancy saturates at 83.0% and will not climb. Spaces held peak at five per kind and fall as capacity shrinks further — because a Trip that cannot park is refused, and a refused Trip is a car that needs no space. "A shed cannot be filled by shrinking it." ⚠ A ratifier defeated by the very thing it was written to observe — and no world fixes this one.

Land value — built, correct, and unobservable

The producer shipped and no baseline moved, where the plan predicted every one would. The reason is worse than the prediction: the only thing in the whole build that creates a Cell row is a pollution emission, and no shipped Ruleset emitted any — all eight said so in their own headers. So the producer walked an empty table in every world that existed.

"The milestone's mechanism is built and unobservable, and it is unobservable for want of Ruleset content rather than for want of code." ⚠ Which also made that milestone's own weight floor unreachable — the same failure arriving one level down, four hours after it was written. "The checklist still does not ask whether the named state can occur, and knowing that it does not ask was not enough to make me ask."

The fix was a tenth Ruleset — and its simplification then broke the reading

A file was authored with one Rule that emits pollution, so the difference between it and its parent is the field and nothing else. ⚠ Its emitting kind is dwelling, which contradicts what the other files say — because a Zone Rule builds only while people are unhoused, so the only demand signal the build has is demand for homes. Industrial demand is unbuilt. And that simplification is exactly what made the ratifier's third reading unreadable: pollution and noise are rank-concordant at 86–100% on every readable Day, because in that world they have the same source.

ACT 4

What is still open

A refuted dial with no successor, a field bounded above by zero, and a decision sitting on the user's desk

§D1

Two desirability weights carry a floor and a debt, and the reachable one is standing in for the unreachable one

The real ratifier needs a consumer nobody has built — the price surface. So each weight got a reachable floor that refutes and never confirms, plus a debt row that stays open. ⚠ "A reachable check standing in the place of an unreachable one is how a number comes to look settled." And the floor then refuted one of its own readings on the first long run.

9

The field is bounded above by zero — it is a disamenity field

Amenity is absent until milestone 15 and shoreline until 24, so desirability ships with its only positive term missing. That is the opposite of the failure the named-hole discipline was built for: "a hole that throws is safe because nothing can read it; desirability now returns plausible numbers, and no assertion can tell that apart from a finished field." A corpus test refuses any document to state the composition without naming the absence — and it must be deleted the day amenity arrives.

7

Why the departure walk is long, and whether it should be bounded

On the congested world: 0 of 3,330 arrival walks exceed the ceiling and 57 of 3,330 departure walks do, the longest at 16.6 minutes — against a whole-shed walk of 4.8 minutes. "A walk more than three times the shed's own diameter is not a shed walk." ⚠ The obvious explanation was refuted in one command: the same city reports 2,364 completed Trips, 0 no-route, 0 beyond budget, 0 stranded. Two questions, and only the second is arguable.

8

The project's name — a decision that fired in silence and is the user's to make

The trigger fired when the save header wrote its magic number, and nobody said so. Both directions are one line and one re-record while the format is unreleased. ⚠ And the long acceptance run did not close the window: it writes and reads saves inside one process against one build, so the first save carried across a build has still not happened.

6

The Evidence window's size is a guess, and its sizing argument cannot be satisfied by any constant

256 entries. One of the two refuting readings is retired — the window fills inside the first Day on the smallest population shipped. The other is unreachable: there is no episode. Every shipped Ruleset inherits a Rule drawing on a Resource nothing produces, so decline never stops. ⚠ And the scaling objection nobody had raised: at 4,000 Citizens 256 entries is just over one Day of condemnations; scaled to a million it is under five Ticks.

8

A save is sized by the city's high-water mark, not by its population — so its size is an instrument in its own right

Over 49 Days the Lots stood flat and the Buildings oscillated with no trend, and the file still grew 1.0% and then stopped. ⚠ The copy rate is carried as a rate and not a duration on purpose — "0.08 ms over 604 KB says nothing about 131.33 MiB and would be quoted as though it did." Projected it is ~18 ms against the ADR's ~10, with the error direction unknown and both signs present. The copy at a million Citizens is owed.

⚠ tests

The suite's cost did not drift — it moved in one commit, on one day

36m 22s, of which 34m 22s is a single test that prices an allocator. The lesson recorded beside it: "a suite's cost is not a slope, and looking for one is what hides the commit that moved it." ⚠ The split into assertion and instrument tiers took all seventeen instruments, ten of which had never been observed being slow — "a list of the slow things you noticed is not a list of the slow things."
