Zoning, one block at a time

Three working interaction sketches. Every stroke snaps to whole blocks, including unequal blocks. Compare the tool browser and commitment gesture; the schematic city and permissions are illustrative.

Common proposal, still open for review

Existing Buildings remain when permissions change or are erased; future development uses the new permissions. Land without a Street can retain permissions and wait for access. Automatic subdivision follows those permissions. None of these sketches implements the simulation.

Try: select a permission, hide Tools, drag across blocks, cancel a stroke with Escape, then try Erase. C holds your selection until Apply. The browser stays open initially for comparison. Roads, Services and Policies show scope notes only.
