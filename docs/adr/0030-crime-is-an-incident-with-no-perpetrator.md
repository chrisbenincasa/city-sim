# Crime is an Incident with a victim and no perpetrator

**Crime is reopened.** `plans/0002` thread E deferred it — *"the richer version, deferred, not the missing piece"* — on the grounds that abandonment alone was a safer decline vector. That deferral is overturned deliberately rather than routed around. Crime ships as **discrete Incidents whose rate derives from unemployment**, dispatching a police response **Trip that can fail**, and **naming no offender**.

## Why the deferral was overturned

Thread E's vector was abandonment, and abandonment has a practical defect it did not anticipate: **it only fires in Districts that are already dead.** A decline mechanism that appears exclusively at the bottom is one nobody can playtest and nobody can intervene in. Unemployment gives the same cycle an **early** entry point, which is the whole reason to have it.

## Why it does not say what thread E feared

Thread E's objection was that a crime system encodes *"poor people cause problems"* where abandonment says *"empty buildings degrade a neighbourhood."* That objection dissolves once the driver is unemployment specifically, because **unemployment in this design is not a property of people.**

Per `CONTEXT` → Destitution: *"Destitution is a reachability failure wearing a money costume. The Household has no money **because** it cannot reach work."* Unemployment is manufactured by the player's network, zoning, and policy. The chain is therefore:

> **reachability failure → unemployment → crime**

which reads *"unreachable jobs cause problems,"* and whose lever is a bus line. Crime and destitution become the same diagnosis with two readouts, which is why they co-locate on the map.

The guardrail, because this is the thing that will drift:

> **Crime reads employment. Never income, never skill tier, never Life Stage.**

A city of fully-employed low-wage tier-1 workers has low crime. A city with unemployed tier-3 workers has crime. Any later proposal that poor Districts are inherently more criminal is caught by that line.

## No perpetrator

An Incident has a **victim, a place, and a response**. There are only two ways to name an offender and both fail a test the corpus already set:

| Selection | Fails |
|---|---|
| Random draw from the unemployed in range | [`0027`](0027-preference-is-drawn-per-household-and-persists-for-life.md)'s standard that *"variation acquires a **cause** rather than a seed"* |
| Deterministic — the most destitute in range | Thread E's objection, rendered as a proper noun: the game accuses the poorest family in the District, every time |

There is no third option, because **no simulated fact distinguishes an offender from a non-offender.** Manufacturing one means a criminality attribute on the Citizen record, which is a far larger claim than the mechanic needs.

`Evidence` is satisfied regardless. Its rule is that a figure expands into *the entities behind it* — and the entities behind a crime rate genuinely **are** the unemployed Households the rate was derived from. Expanding it yields 310 names, a District, and a set of jobs nobody can reach. The chain is fully navigable; it simply contains no accusation.

It is also the cheap answer, since a named offender would have to **travel** to the crime — an activity model for criminals. The honest model and the fast one coincide, as they did for the school run.

## Police suppress the symptom, never the cause

Response reachability lowers the Incident rate. It does nothing to vacancy and nothing to unemployment. **Policing a declining District buys a quieter decline, not a recovery** — which preserves thread E's actual purpose: *"neglect must not be containable, or ignoring a poor District is free."*

The player's counterplay to crime is therefore never police. It is the levers that refill empty buildings and put jobs back in range — rezoning, remediation, a District tax override, running transit in — every one of which `01-player §5` already lists as the recovery path for a dead District.

**Presence counts, and it counts twice.** Safety and **intrusion** are both `0027` Taste axes reading the same police density; safety saturates with diminishing returns and intrusion does not, so the two curves cross. Nobody authors where the crossover sits, and it sits in a different place for every Household — which makes over-policing a real cost rather than a flavour note, and is `CONTEXT` → Policy's incidence rule appearing somewhere nobody expected: *there is no setting that favours everyone.*

## Consequences

- **Crime is an entry point to the existing decline cycle, not a second decline system.** Incidents drive Businesses out → jobs and Amenity fall → unemployment rises → more Incidents. Damped at the bottom by bid price, exactly as abandonment is. A cycle, not a spiral.
- **Fire and police become one mechanism** — dispatch reachability producing a response Trip and an ambient term — which is most of ledger #14c.
- **The incident rate's constant is in domain units**: *incidents per unemployed Household per Day*. A designer can defend it and a player can read it off a panel.
- **Crime types are deferred** with a stated trigger. See `deferred.md`.
