---
description: Put a decision to the user as prose with costs and trade-offs
priority: 45
---
- When a choice needs the user, write it out: the problem, the options, what each one costs, and the
  trade-off that separates them. Lead with a recommendation and say what drives it.
- Avoid `AskUserQuestion` for anything whose meaning does not fit in a short label. It has been
  rejected far more often than it has been answered here.
- Reserve the picker for a genuinely enumerable choice where the labels carry the whole decision.
- Batch open questions into one message at a natural boundary rather than interrupting per question.
