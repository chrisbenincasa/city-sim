At a natural pause, judge whether a concrete weakness has earned an improvement. Keep what works and
do not change the workspace for novelty. Decide the benefit, the approach and the boundary before
implementing. Check first whether the existing corpus, the installed plugins, the project skills
(`next`, `drive`) or an existing instrument already covers it, and improve that before proposing
anything parallel. Offer `/quartermaster:resupply` only with current or standing approval, and every
recommended change still needs its own.
- **A claim you could not check** (correct? fast enough? complete?) may need a measurement. See
  whether an existing instrument answers it before building one, and route a cost to `plans/0013`.
- **A multi-step job done by hand** may belong in a project skill.
- **Something re-derived or re-explored** may belong in the document that owns it.
- **A convention you had to be told** may belong in a tightly-scoped live rule.
- **A finding with no home** gets routed on the day (`adr/0073`): a defect to the code or
  `plans/0003`, a question to `plans/0002`, a document now wrong to `plans/0012`.
Keep it to one evidenced improvement. If the existing capabilities hold up, say so plainly.
