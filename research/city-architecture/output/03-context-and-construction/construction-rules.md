# Construction rules for the research models

These are proposed modelling constraints informed by the [atlas](atlas/index.html), not construction certification. The [roof plate](drawings/construction.svg), [workplace](drawings/workplace.svg) and [evidence controls](drawings/evidence-controls.svg) show their consequences.

| Decision | Can vary relatively independently | Must change together | Invalid combination |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wall finish | Colour, staining, compatible repair patch | Thickness/returns, sill projection, joints and mounting | Brick texture on arbitrary panel dimensions without corners or module control |
| Openings | Some infill/replacement detail | Room/core locations, bearing piers, lintels, shutter clearance | Window rhythm claiming occupied rooms behind stair cores or solid party walls |
| Residential length | Number of repeated bays within a chosen family | Entrances, stairs, dwellings/content mapping and structural breaks | Doubling a house width and enlarging every room/window |
| Residential depth | Small bounded adjustments | Daylight, circulation, structure and service cores | Deep plan declared residential solely because it has perimeter windows |
| Pitched roof | Finish within its compatible pitch/system | Span, pitch, rise, eaves, gable, covering and drainage | Low membrane range fitted with steep-roof clay details because it is “European” |
| Flat-looking roof | Parapet material/cap detail | Deck, insulation, membrane, falls, low points, overflow, curbs | Horizontal plate with unrelated roof machinery and no route for water |
| Industrial hall | Bay repetition | Primary frame, purlins/deck, bracing, cladding and clearances | Purlins treated as primary 24 m beams; doorway through necessary bracing |
| Upper workplace floor | Internal lightweight partitions | Full floor support, load paths, stairs and services | Partial mezzanine rendered where two full simulation floors are counted |
| Mixed-use corner | Signs and shopfront repair | Independent thresholds, vertical circulation and service path | Deliveries through the residential stair; balcony occupying unallocated ground |
| Party edge | Blank wall surface and compatible fire stopping detail | Roof abutment, drainage and opening permissions | Free-standing eave/window protruding through a neighbour |
| Court / setback | Surface within already allocated open ground | Footprint, floor area, access and capacity | Court cut out of a solid counted plate; smaller body floating inside owned ground |

Source relationships: [DK2](atlas/index.html#DK2) supplies low-house construction logic; [DK3](atlas/index.html#DK3) distinguishes bearing and nonbearing facades; [C1](atlas/index.html#C1) separates structural scales; [CA5](atlas/index.html#CA5) supplies real roof junctions; [ES1](atlas/index.html#ES1) demonstrates another roof assembly. These are transferable mechanisms, not national exclusivities.

## Actual roof proposal parameters

| Model | Authored geometry | Meaning / limit |
|---|---|---|
| Detached study | 10 m span, 25°, calculated rise 2.332 m | Truss study, not surveyed house or certified timber span |
| Range construction study | 12 m span, 11°, rise 1.166 m | Uses documented Tingbjerg angle with a proposed span; membrane assembly |
| Workshop construction study | 24 m span, 6°, rise 1.261 m | Indicative portal study; needs frame sizing; no implied full upper floor |
| G001 low roof | 10 m drainage run, 1:60, rise 0.167 m | Proposed tapered assembly above a level structure; parapet and overflow need detailed levels |
| Material scale | UK 215×65 mm brick face; proposed 10 mm joints | Product dimension plus art choice, not regional module standard |

The roof-assembly plate uses enlarged exploded layers and labels that scale exception. Other sections state pixels per metre. A door is 0.9×2.1 m and a person 1.7 m in the drawings: authored scale references, not accessibility compliance or a population distribution.

The [dated Bauder commentary](atlas/index.html#C2) conflicts with its [general web page](atlas/index.html#C3). Retain both; neither becomes a game regulation. Carlisle's quarter-inch-per-foot example is exactly 1:48, not exactly 1:50. Most important for the drawing is that every low point has an outlet and every curb has a drainage solution.

## Levels of model detail

Silhouette: body, actual roof, major access, deep recesses, attached service volumes. Near view: doors, stairs, gutters, parapet caps, structural bay cues and large seams. Material view: brick courses, membrane seams, panel joints and local weathering. Do not inflate physical material modules merely to make them visible; compare true scale with a separately labelled stylisation.

Instance repetition, triangle counts and draw cost remain unmeasured. This package authors research geometry only; it makes no production performance claim.
