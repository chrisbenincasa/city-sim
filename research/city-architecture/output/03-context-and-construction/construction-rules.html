The roof-assembly plate uses enlarged exploded layers and labels that scale exception. Other sections state pixels per metre. A door is 0.9×2.1 m and a person 1.7 m in the drawings: authored scale references, not accessibility compliance or a population distribution.

The dated Bauder commentary conflicts with its general web page. Retain both; neither becomes a game regulation. Carlisle's quarter-inch-per-foot example is exactly 1:48, not exactly 1:50. Most important for the drawing is that every low point has an outlet and every curb has a drainage solution.

Levels of model detail

Silhouette: body, actual roof, major access, deep recesses, attached service volumes. Near view: doors, stairs, gutters, parapet caps, structural bay cues and large seams. Material view: brick courses, membrane seams, panel joints and local weathering. Do not inflate physical material modules merely to make them visible; compare true scale with a separately labelled stylisation.

Instance repetition, triangle counts and draw cost remain unmeasured. This package authors research geometry only; it makes no production performance claim.
