# Review guide

Start at [the illustrated review](index.html). Open SVGs at full size if the inline preview is too small. The first drawings are monochrome; the material treatment is separate.

1. **Apartment comparison:** follow the stairs in plan and find the corresponding doors in elevation. Decide whether a shallower range and a deeper corridor block both belong in the desired city. They are not national definitions.
2. **Five streets:** compare Sacramento's low bodies, South Bend's incremental plots, Amersfoort's rows, Tingbjerg's shared gardens and the provisional Mediterranean block. Choose relationships, not labels. The southern block has weaker local plan evidence.
3. **Fictional mixture:** inspect front-to-side transitions and the service lane. Is the mixture coherent enough to be the starting context? No answer is presumed.
4. **Workplace:** find the complete upper floor, cores, receiving area, roof outlets and plant. Judge whether the large game body now reads as a plausible building family.
5. **Materials/history:** inspect warmth, slate, modern shopfronts and restrained changes over time only after geometry.

The choices needing your judgment are the fictional starting street, the desired balance of open versus continuous frontage, visual variation within a neighbourhood, and whether named contexts are worth keeping as player choices. A slider is not yet recommended.

Content questions are separate: how architectural homes map to tenancies, what use large bodies carry, and how extensions/courts/service ground are represented. The twelve-flat A1 is explicitly not a silent replacement for G003's two-tenancy ceiling.

This package contains completed research proposals and evidence comparisons. It does not contain a production asset library, approved architectural plans, a new simulation run or a player-tested regional selector. See [validation](VALIDATION.html) for exact checks and limits.
