## H1 — attached housing

Repeat four 6×12 m modules with aligned party walls. Reserve a 2×3 m stair zone within each; place the entrance/service rooms toward the street, larger living openings and doors toward private rear ground. Use a roof spanning the 12 m depth, not one roof spanning the 24 m frontage. Initial pitch 18° is an art proposal; covering must be chosen compatibly. Test warm brick, painted timber or panel infill separately. Draw end walls differently from party walls.

Constraints: [US1](atlas/index.html#US1) supplies access/attached-plan controls; [NL1](atlas/index.html#NL1) supplies a module warning, not the proposed 6 m external width; [DK2](atlas/index.html#DK2) supplies front/garden logic. No additional doors are interpreted as extra simulated Households.

## Review protocol and stopping point

1. Match orthographic scale and camera, neutral materials and lighting. Identify every public/stair/service entrance from both sides. Reject roof geometry that changes between plan, elevation and axon.
2. Compare H1/A1/A2 unlabelled for silhouette and access; this tests whether the differences communicate, not nationality recognition.
3. Inspect the rear lane and gap transitions; trace each service route. Keep garden, court and receiving ground visible. Vehicle swept paths need a later actual check.
4. Apply the separate warm/slate material pass. Then make one repaired shopfront, one roof repair and one restrained near-future intervention on the same base geometry.
5. Only after that preference review, decide whether to implement one exact-body W1 and an explicitly capacity-labelled G003 study in Godot. If doing so, use drive and build Debug before captures; review near, neighbourhood, city, moving camera and overlays.

Completed here: source constraints, scaled research drawings, body/ground schedule and review criteria. Unperformed: production meshes, detailed room layout, engineering/egress compliance, simulation integration, Godot performance, moving-camera review and user recognition testing.
