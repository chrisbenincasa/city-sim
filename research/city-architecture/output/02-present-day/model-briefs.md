# Three contemporary research models

**All N1–N3 dimensions below are PROPOSED, in metres.** Sources constrain the reasoning; these models do not reproduce surveyed buildings. No occupancy, engineering approval or regulatory compliance is implied. First model the untextured bodies and entrances. Regional treatments are alternative assemblies with compatible geometry, not textures that can conceal a different plan.

## Shared construction rules

| Decision | Rule for this prototype | Evidence and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Widening | Repeat complete dwelling/structural bays; preserve stair and wet-service locations | South Bend plans and Dutch bay schedules; not independent random widths |
| Deepening | Add an explicit circulation/support arrangement; use a different family if rooms cannot reach an exterior | South Bend stepped plan; Wohnregal's different long-span structure |
| Flat roof | Deck, fall, waterproofing, upstand/coping, outlet, overflow and maintenance access form one assembly | Seattle roof plan locates functions; falls below remain proposed because a built section was not obtained |
| Pitched roof | Span, pitch, covering, supporting members and eaves are coupled; any valley requires a drainage route | No national pitch interpolation; US and Dutch references both admit alternatives |
| Support | House floors use party/internal walls; apartment uses a frame plus reserved cores; workshop uses its own frame family | [Wohnregal](https://publikationen.bibliothek.kit.edu/1000125224/90508743#page=2); [portal-frame anatomy](https://steelconstruction.info/topics/design/portal-frames) |
| Doors | Every intended dwelling or common lobby has a traceable access path; shop and home entrances stay separate | [Seattle ground plan](https://www.seattle.gov/dpd/AppDocs/GroupMeetings/DRProposal3012121AgendaID4348.pdf#page=13) |
| Materials | Draw construction modules at real scale; colour, roughness and repair vary within them | Brick 215 × 102.5 × 65 mm and plain tile 265 × 165 mm are retained product controls, not universal regional sizes |
| Condition | Keep load-bearing form and access; vary patching, coatings, replaced joinery and service interventions | Abandoned state is not evidence of structural collapse |

Metsä's first T-element table row is a useful caution: a 225 mm-deep, five-rib element is listed for a 7 m roof span but 5.25 m residential floor span. The page assumes particular roof/ceiling/screed/partition loads and excludes fire resistance, under NL/BE annex conditions. Neither span certifies our proposed floors. [Metsä, p 25](https://www.metsagroup.com/globalassets/metsa-wood/attachments/others/metsa-kerto-ripa-prefabricated-building-elements.pdf.pdf#page=25)

## N1 — four attached contemporary homes

**Test:** a long range reads as individual homes through entrances and structural rhythm, with modest roof mass. US01/02 are narrower-house plan controls; NL01 and NL03 supply contemporary row relationships. Their differing inside/outside conventions are retained in the atlas. Choosing 6 m exterior frontage below is a game-model decision, not their averaged measurement.

- **Ground:** four 6 × 12 homes; main range 24 × 12. Two full storeys; levels 0,3,6. Plot per home 6 × 24: front threshold 2, building 12, rear garden 10. Rear lane 2 wide is additional ground beyond the plots. End homes alone have side windows; maintain an unobstructed route from each rear door to the lane.
- **Plan/support:** reserve 0.30 exterior wall and 0.25 party wall. Side hall 1.2 wide; stair reservation 2 × 4 beside party wall, landing 1.2. A transverse support line halfway along the depth divides the floor arrangement; size members later. A front living room and rear kitchen/dining zone face the two exterior elevations. Bathrooms/services stack. These are spatial reservations, not furnished or approved floor plans.
- **Front:** two 3 m bays per home, centres 1.5 and 4.5. Entrance 1.0 × 2.2 in the left bay. Ground window 1.5 × 1.5 in the right; upper windows 1.2 × 1.5 at both centres. Upper sills 0.85 above floor. Recess 0.15, threshold 0.05. No door/window interpolation under the regional control.
- **Pitched assembly option:** ridge parallel to street; 12 m wall span at 25° gives rise `6×tan(25°)=2.798`, ridge 8.798. Use a proposed standing-seam metal covering; 0.30 eaves follow the same slope. Internal supports and roof framing must be visible in the construction blockout. Gutters 0.15 wide front/rear; downpipes 0.08 at selected party lines. No gratuitous chimney on every new home.
- **Flat assembly option:** replace the complete pitched assembly with deck at 6, parapet 0.35, proposed falls 1:40 across two 6 m runs, fall 0.15 to a collection line and two outlets/overflows. This is a distinct model variant, not the midpoint of the pitched variant. Do not count an extra habitable roof floor in either option.
- **Regional treatment:** US-informed version adds a 1.5-deep front porch within the 2 m strip, with warm siding over a ventilated assembly and a masonry base. Dutch-informed version uses a smaller 0.9 canopy and brick facing with coloured joinery. Both are original choices; both may use either roof assembly after checking junctions. The mixed street selects complete home/range treatments while retaining party-wall and roof continuity.
- **Back:** one selected unit receives a later 3 × 2 ground-floor addition, walls 2.7 and shallow sheet roof. This covers previously open plot ground and therefore needs an eventual simulation/content decision. Its gutter cannot discharge into the next home's wall.

**Unresolved:** four physical dwellings on a 24 m range need a deliberate Building/Lot mapping. Four 6 m Lots do not align with the current 4 m Tile grid. Do not disguise that by relabelling every front door a separately simulated Address.

## N2 — contemporary corner shops and apartments

**Test:** the corner has a usable ground floor, independent residential access and believable roof services, without nineteenth-century ornament. Seattle's live/work proposal is an access/level/material control, not a direct corner-shop reconstruction; cb 19 demonstrates a contemporary mixed-use programme and distinct service side.

- **Ground:** 16 × 12 body; primary street along the 16 m side, secondary street at the right return. Levels 0,4.2,7.35,10.5. Counted architectural gross floor is 576 m². Roof parapet rises another 0.45.
- **Ground layout:** reserve two shop envelopes, left 8 × 12 and right 8 × 12. In the right envelope reserve a 4 × 6 residential stair/lobby core against the right street edge, with independent 1.2 × 2.2 door on that return. The shops each retain a primary-front door; nominal usable shop area is deliberately unstated because walls, core and service rooms must be deducted. Rear service doors reach a 4 m service strip beyond the body.
- **Upper layout:** two dwelling envelopes per level, separated at the 8 m line. Residential core continues vertically and connects to a 1.5-wide landing in the same reservation; service stacks at the rear. Four proposed dwellings in total, two shops. Do not infer how many Households, Businesses or jobs would occupy them.
- **Structure/front:** proposed frame at 4 m intervals along frontage, with transverse support lines at depths 0,6,12. Ground shopfronts follow 4 m bays: piers 0.30, stallriser 0.45, glazing 2.6 high, fascia 0.45 within the 4.2 m floor band. Upper openings 1.2 × 1.5 arranged around room/core positions; no transparent stair shaft through a shop tenancy.
- **Roof:** low-slope waterproofed assembly at 10.5; 1:40 falls over 6 m runs create 0.15 fall. Reserve a 3 × 3 headhouse over the core, 2.4 high, and a separate 3 × 4 equipment zone with access route 1.0 wide. Coping, upstands, two primary drains and two visible overflow positions must appear in the roof plan. PV is a later variant with its own support and clearance reservations.
- **Regional treatment:** warm brick and grey metal/wood fascia in the US-informed version; buff/red brick, more continuous upper-floor masonry and coloured recessed joinery in the Dutch-informed version. Roof equipment and the entrance hierarchy remain. Proposed material seam module 0.6 is an authoring simplification, not the exact 24-inch Seattle schedule value.

**Exclusions:** no invented full retail frontage on service walls; no delivery truck entering through the residential lobby; no blind party wall against required side access. This new research footprint is not claimed to reproduce one of the five selected game Buildings.

## N3 — apartment block on G003's exact body

**Test:** the existing 24 × 16 × 10.5 m wall body reads as an apartment building with a resolved low-slope roof. Keep its 1,152 m² counted floor. References US03/05 and DE02/04 demonstrate multiple apartment/access/structural arrangements, not a measured twelve-unit match to this footprint.

- **Floors and frame:** 0,3.5,7,10.5. Proposed 6 × 8 frame grid with brick infill/facing and reserved cores. Neither the 13 m Wohnregal span nor a timber roof-span table licenses an unspecified 16 m floor span.
- **Circulation:** central 24 × 2 corridor; two end stair reservations 4 × 5 overlap it by 4 × 2 each. Central front lobby 2 × 7 reaches the corridor. Union area is `48+40−16+14=86 m²` per floor. Reserve the same front strip on upper floors for shared/service use. Remaining area is 298 m²/floor, averaging 74.5 m² per four proposed apartment envelopes **before walls and shafts**; not net dwelling area. Twelve dwellings total remains a layout hypothesis.
- **Access:** front door 1.2 × 2.2; canopy 2.4 × 1.2 within allocated front ground. End stair doors lead through 2.4 m side paths to street and rear garden. Reserve a 2.2 × 2.2 lift within the west core; detailed stair/lift fitting and access compliance remain unresolved before production.
- **Elevation:** eight 3 m principal bays; windows 1.2 × 1.5, sill 0.85 above each floor; central lobby replaces the two inner ground windows. End/core elevations express stairs. US, Dutch and mixed treatments retain those openings and supports in the controlled comparison; only selected wall panels, joinery, entrance details and shading change. This intentionally isolates regional treatment from density and floor area.
- **Roof:** deck 10.5, parapet 0.45 high/0.20 thick. Proposed 1:40 transverse falls over 8 m give 0.20 level difference, meeting a longitudinal collector divided into two drainage catchments. Two outlets and overflow positions; waterproof upstands at all penetrations. A 2 × 2 headhouse rises 2.4 over the east stair; roof access path 1.0. Upper section in the scale sheet cuts midspan, so it does not include the headhouse.
- **Near-future variant:** add an explicitly reserved PV zone and lightweight external shading to an unchanged frame. Re-cladding adds 0.15 outside the original wall plane: test this separately and account for plot/side-path encroachment. A balcony or winter garden would need new supported ground/cantilever and access checks; do not slip it into a material-only comparison.

**Content mismatch:** archived G003 permits two shared tenancies; twelve architectural dwellings do not become twelve simulated Households by drawing them. Resolve interpretation and content separately. Retain the body in all three regional comparisons.

## Prototype street and review

Use a proposed scene **80 × 72**, with coordinates x=−10…70, y=−34…38. Ground is level for the first test. Primary road occupies y=−10…−4, pavements y=−4…0 and y=−14…−10. North building frontage is y=2 after a 2 m threshold; south frontage is y=−16 after a 2 m threshold.

Place N1 at x=0…24,y=2…14; N2 at x=24…40,y=2…14, with its right street/service access beyond x=40. Gardens/service ground occupy y=14…24; rear lane y=24…26. Place N3 at x=4…28,y=−32…−16, with side paths at x=1.6…4 and 28…30.4. A proposed **18 × 12, 5 m-wall workshop** can sit at x=46…64,y=14…26; give it a 6 m-deep van apron to the south. Its 6° roof and portal-frame concept are provisional; no heavy-truck turning proof or industrial capacity is claimed.

1. Compare three matched scenes: US-informed, Dutch-informed, fictional mixture. Same use, floor count, plots, sun and camera. Do not change every roof merely to strengthen the difference.
2. Review plan, street-eye at 1.75 m, oblique neighbourhood and city views, then a moving camera. Count intended entrances before reading labels; inspect backs, service access and roof junctions.
3. Compare the original G003 body/roof beside N3 at identical scale. Check whether the access and roof assembly explain the size.
4. Compare complete materials at true modules; then occupied/abandoned condition under matched day/night and overlays. This is a later Godot experiment requiring the drive skill and a Debug build.
5. Add one older survivor as an optional infill replacement and one postwar retrofit version. These are deliberate test cases, not a stock-frequency model. Keep the regional blend fixed while testing age.

Reuse complete party-wall units, frame bays, opening assemblies and roof drainage modules with consistent pivots. Keep silhouette, close construction detail, surface material and condition separate. No performance claim is established until measured in the actual shell.
