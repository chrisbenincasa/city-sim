## Why retrieval stopped

Every agreed question has primary evidence or an explicit limit, dimensional controls exist for the immediate models, and consequential source contradictions are bounded. Further broad searches would mainly add regional examples without deciding whether the player likes the mixture. Remaining workshop/retail surveys, as-built roof sections and missing climates are named follow-up targets. Stop here and evaluate the bounded prototype before expanding geographic coverage or claiming prevalence.
