What this prototype is testing

Stable category positions; one left workspace; tool options beside the active tool; inspection preserved while acting; layers that outlive their picker. Search spans all families. Stars create fixed-order favourites for this session.

Catalogue badges describe the study, not a full implementation audit: Sketch means a future interaction placeholder; Unresolved means scope or interaction design still needs a decision. All figures and consequences here are illustrative. No save, simulation, construction cost, or balance claim is made.

Keyboard: Tab to navigate, Enter to activate; Escape cancels a stroke, then closes the catalogue, then cancels the tool. Map parcels work with Enter or Space. Hover or focus a parcel for feedback. At 1280 × 800 and up, the study uses the desktop composition; smaller screens scroll a minimum-width study.

References: Factorio quickbar, information navigation, Cities: Skylines II road tools, Anno menu growth.
