What this prototype is testing

Place and edit on the left: Connections, Zoning, Municipal, Utilities and Demolish. Govern and inspect from the bottom console. Government has a separator from the data buttons because its policies change the city.

Municipal groups education, health, parks and recreation, police and fire. Utilities groups water, power, sewage and waste. These are menu groupings, not changes to the simulation's Service taxonomy. Each compact chooser fits its contents; selecting a placeable tool folds it away. Zoning retains six direct permissions and separate Erase, without search or favourites.

Government opens a grouped chooser for finance, Wards, programmes, Transit operation and unresolved land interventions. Opening it preserves the held tool, active layer and inspected subject. Selecting a policy opens its placeholder in the inspector; Transit operation retains the working sample interaction. Compare the previous study using the link above.

Badges describe the study, not an implementation audit: Sketch means a future placeholder; Unresolved means scope or interaction design remains open. All figures and outcomes are illustrative.

Keyboard: Tab and Enter navigate; Escape cancels a stroke, closes the latest open chooser, then cancels the tool. Map parcels accept Enter or Space. At 1280 × 800 and up, the study uses the desktop composition; smaller screens scroll a minimum-width study.

Reference: SimCity manual — system information and data maps. It describes category-related data maps and a general map chooser. This arrangement is our exploration.
