What this prototype is testing

Tools live on the left; data views launch from the bottom console. Small tool choosers fit their contents and fold away after selection. Zoning has six direct permissions, including a provisional Mixed use choice, plus Erase. No search or favourites are attached to zoning. Larger tool families retain their catalogue controls.

Tool selection, data views and inspection keep independent state. Maps close after selection and leave a compact legend. Pins track inspected subjects, separately from catalogue favourites. Compare the previous study using the link above.

Badges describe the study, not an implementation audit: Sketch means a future placeholder; Unresolved means scope or interaction design remains open. All figures and outcomes are illustrative.

Keyboard: Tab and Enter navigate; Escape cancels a stroke, closes the latest open chooser, then cancels the tool. Map parcels accept Enter or Space. At 1280 × 800 and up, the study uses the desktop composition; smaller screens scroll a minimum-width study.

Reference: SimCity manual — system information and data maps. It describes category-related data maps and a general map chooser. The specific left-tools / bottom-data arrangement here is our exploration.
